What it is

Pay in Bitcoin Cash. Get 3% back, guaranteed by a contract.

Shops that accept BCH reward repeat customers with stamps. Stamps are redeemable for BCH from a reserve the shop funds up front and cannot take back from a customer.

The story

Card networks take about 3% of every sale. BCH takes a fraction of a cent.

A shop that accepts Bitcoin Cash keeps that 3%, but its customers have no reason to care. Regulars hands the saving to the customer as cashback, which gives people a reason to choose BCH at the till and a reason to come back.

Ordinary loyalty points are a promise the shop can change or cancel. Here the reward money is locked on-chain before the first stamp is issued.

BCH usage

Three mainnet transactions, one covenant

  1. Pay. A plain BCH payment to the shop, signed in the customer's wallet over WizardConnect.
  2. Earn. The shop sends stamps, a CashTokens fungible token, to the paying address. Issuing spends the payment's own output, so a payment can never be rewarded twice.
  3. Redeem. The customer hands stamps to the RewardReserve covenant and takes BCH out. The script checks the reserve is recreated, the stamp count grew by N, and at most N × rate left.

Written in CashScript, tested on mock network, chipnet and mainnet, with browser tests that drive a headless WizardConnect wallet.

The vision

A rewards layer for every BCH merchant

  • Now: one demo shop live on mainnet.
  • Next: self-serve onboarding so any shop can mint stamps and fund a reserve in minutes, with a plug-in for existing BCH point-of-sale apps.
  • Business: a monthly fee per shop or a small cut of reserve funding. The shop is paying out of card fees it no longer owes.
  • Later: rewards priced in a BCH stablecoin, and an XO template so wallets can show and redeem stamps natively.
