What it is

Pay a shop in Bitcoin Cash. Your reward arrives in the same transaction.

Any shop can open a loyalty programme in one transaction from its own wallet. Customers earn stamps the instant they pay, and redeem them for BCH from a reserve the shop locks up front.

Two contracts and a static web page. No server, no database, no keys held by anyone but the shop and its customers.

The story

Card networks take about 3% of every sale. BCH takes a fraction of a cent.

A shop that accepts Bitcoin Cash keeps that 3%, but its customers have no reason to care. Regulars hands the saving to the customer as cashback, which gives people a reason to choose BCH at the till and a reason to come back.

Ordinary loyalty points are a promise the shop can change or cancel. Here the reward money is locked on-chain before the first stamp is issued.

BCH usage

Two covenants do the work a company's servers usually do

  1. StampIssuer. Holds the shop's unissued stamps (a CashTokens fungible token). Anyone may take N stamps, but only in a transaction whose output 1 pays the shop for them. Payment and reward are atomic.
  2. RewardReserve. Holds the shop's reward BCH. Anyone handing in N stamps takes N × rate out. The shop cannot refuse.
  3. Protocol fee, enforced in script. Each issuance must leave 1% inside the issuer contract, which only the protocol treasury can collect.

CashScript, signed in the user's wallet over WizardConnect. Tested on mock network, chipnet and mainnet, including browser tests that drive a headless WizardConnect wallet through paying, redeeming and opening a shop.

The vision

The rewards layer for every BCH merchant

  • Now: live on mainnet. Anyone can open a shop today and print its poster.
  • Business: 1% of every rewarded payment accrues to the protocol on-chain, with no invoicing and no accounts. Shops pay it out of the roughly 3% they no longer give to card networks.
  • Next: an XO template so wallets show and redeem stamps natively, and a plug-in for existing BCH point-of-sale apps.
  • Later: rewards priced in a BCH stablecoin, and a public directory of shops read straight from the chain.
